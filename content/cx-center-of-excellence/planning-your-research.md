Carefully planning is essential for successful customer experience research. This page guides you through key conciderations when designing you research study.

## What to consider in this stage of the process?

### How many participants do you need?

The number of participants directly impacts your PRA requirements and research approach:

- **Fewer than 10 participants**: May not require PRA clearance (see [When PRA Applies](/cx-center-of-excellence/when-pra-applies/))
- **10 or more participants**: Will likely require PRA clearance

Consider:
- What sample size do you need for reliable insights?
- Can qualitative methods with fewer participants answer your questions?
- Would multiple small studies be more effective than one large study?

### What research methods are appropriate?

Choose methods that:
- Match your research questions
- Fit your timeline and resources
- Are appropriate for your participants
- Comply with PRA requirements

Explore [Research Methods](/cx-center-of-excellence/methods/) to learn more.

## Considerations for Paperwork Reduction Act

### The 10-participant threshold

**If your research study requires more than 10 participants it will require you to navigate the Paperwork Reduction Act.**

Key PRA planning considerations:

1. **Timeline**: PRA clearance can take 6-12+ months
  - [Fast Track Process](/cx-center-of-excellence/generic-fast-track-approval-process/): ~60 days (if eligible)
  - [Normal Clearance](/cx-center-of-excellence/normal-clearance-approval-process/): 6-12+ months

2. **Study design**: Plan your study with PRA in mind
  - [How to Conduct Research with PRA in Mind](/cx-center-of-excellence/how-to-conduct-with-pra-in-mind/)
  - Consider breaking larger studies into smaller phases

3. **Documentation**: Prepare required materials early
  - [Clearance Types](/cx-center-of-excellence/clearance-types/)
  - Supporting statements and burden estimates

## Research Planning Checklist

Before proceeding, ensure you have:

- [ ] Defined clear research questions
- [ ] Determined your target participant count
- [ ] Checked the [Research Repository](/cx-center-of-excellence/research-repository/)
- [ ] Selected appropriate [research methods](/cx-center-of-excellence/methods/)
- [ ] Assessed [PRA requirements](/cx-center-of-excellence/when-pra-applies/)
- [ ] Reviewed [compiance requirements](/cx-center-of-excellence/compliance/)
- [ ] Considered [equity and inclusion](/cx-center-of-excellence/cx-and-equitable-research/)
- [ ] Planned for [participant recruitment](/cx-center-of-excellence/participant-recruitment/)
- [ ] Budgeted for [participant compensation](/cx-center-of-excellence/participant-compensation/) (if applicable)
